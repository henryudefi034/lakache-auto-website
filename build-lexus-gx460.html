THE PROJECT
STORY.

The GX460 arrived with significant rear-impact damage. The collision affected more than the bodywork — the damage also reached the rear drivetrain, including the differential and driver-side axle, leaving the vehicle unable to move normally.

The first stage is being handled at the body shop so the damaged rear structure can be repaired and the vehicle can move again. Once that work is complete, the GX returns to the Lakache Auto project lineup for the mechanical phase: replacing the differential and rear axle components and working toward getting this GX460 properly back on the road.

WHY THIS BUILD

A SOLID GX.
ONE BAD CORNER.

From the front, the Lexus presents like a remarkably complete SUV. The story changes at the rear, where the collision concentrated the damage and turned an otherwise strong GX460 into the next Lakache Auto recovery project.

The rear body repair comes first. After the structure is restored enough for the vehicle to move, attention turns underneath: the damaged differential and driver-side rear axle are the major mechanical items on the list.
