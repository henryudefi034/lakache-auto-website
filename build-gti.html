THE BUILD
STORY.

The GTI arrived as an auction salvage project and became one of the defining builds on Lakache Auto. The project covered major mechanical and front-end work, transmission installation, body repair, paint and the troubleshooting that followed.

The finished car is also compared with the earlier Mk7 GTI — connecting the original Lakache Auto project car with the newer Mk8 generation.
