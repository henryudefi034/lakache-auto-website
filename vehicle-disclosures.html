Vehicle Disclosures

What to verify before making any decision about a documented salvage or rebuilt vehicle.

Title status and registration

Some listed vehicles have salvage-branded title histories. A salvage brand is not equivalent to a clean title, and repair work does not by itself establish a rebuilt title or roadworthiness. Request current official title documents and confirm registration, inspection and export eligibility for the intended jurisdiction.

Accident and repair history

Our listings may show auction damage, owner-performed repairs, parts replaced and video documentation. A documented repair story is not a comprehensive damage assessment or warranty. Additional prior damage, hidden defects or unrepaired issues may exist.

Mileage and history reports

A vehicle-history snapshot summarizes selected third-party records as of a stated report date; it is not the full report or a current inspection. Historical mileage may differ from the current odometer. Any recorded discrepancies or possible rollback flags should be independently investigated.

Recalls, diagnostics and safety systems

A recall listed in a history report does not necessarily establish whether a remedy is still open. Check manufacturer or NHTSA recall status using the VIN. Verify airbags, seatbelts, ADAS calibration, warning lights, diagnostics, brakes and structural condition with qualified professionals.

Independent inspection encouraged

Prospective eligible buyers should arrange an independent inspection and review photos, repair records, title documents, current odometer evidence and any remaining known issues before agreeing to terms.

Wholesale and export restrictions

Lakache Auto holds a California wholesale-only dealer license. We assess transaction eligibility and applicable legal requirements before proceeding. International buyers should independently confirm destination-country import restrictions, title acceptance, customs duties, taxes, shipping costs and clearance requirements.

Prices and availability

Advertised prices and vehicle availability may change. Listings do not include a promise of shipping, customs clearance, delivery, registration or a completed sale. Any actual terms must be documented separately.
