04 / WHY GOODS?

CARS
BUILD LIFE.

The merchandise is meant to extend the same idea behind Lakache Auto. Cars create projects, skills, conversations, memories and communities.

Lakache Goods should feel connected to that culture—not like a logo placed on random products.

That is why the first collection stays small and intentional while the designs and fulfillment are developed properly.
