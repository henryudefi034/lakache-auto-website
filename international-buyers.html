FOR NIGERIA AND OTHER MARKETS

Questions worth asking before buying.

Title and vehicle history

Ask for the exact title branding, VIN, ownership records and details of prior damage. A salvage title may affect eligibility and value in the destination country.

Repair and current condition

Review what was repaired, which inspections or calibrations are confirmed, and whether any issues remain. Independent inspections are encouraged.

Export documentation

Vehicle export is subject to U.S. Customs documentation, ownership verification and port procedures. Shipping or export arrangements are not included automatically.

Import and landing costs

Confirm destination-country import eligibility, duties, customs clearance, port charges and local registration rules with qualified providers before making a commitment.
