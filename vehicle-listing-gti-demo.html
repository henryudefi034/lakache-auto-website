THE DOCUMENTED STORY

BUILT
IN PUBLIC.

The GTI arrived damaged and became a multi-stage project involving mechanical work, front-end assembly, body repair, paint and diagnosis. The point of a Lakache listing is simple: the buyer should be able to understand the car's past before discussing its future.

01 / ACQUIRED

Purchased as a damaged auction vehicle and documented from the beginning of the project.

02 / MECHANICAL

Transmission installation and front-end assembly were major stages in returning the car to operation.

03 / BODY + PAINT

Body repair and paint moved the GTI from damaged project to a finished exterior.

04 / DIAGNOSIS

A lingering ABS problem was ultimately traced to the front wheel-bearing magnetic encoder ring.

05 / FINISHED

The completed Mk8 returned to the road and became the modern counterpart to the original Lakache Mk7 GTI.
