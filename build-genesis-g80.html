THE PROJECT
STORY.

The G80 entered the Lakache Auto garage as a non-runner after collision damage. Getting the car moving again is only the beginning: the project is about methodically working through the damaged systems and bringing the car back together one repair at a time.

With the project now underway, the next phase includes the replacement headlight, servicing and verifying the cooling system, checking for leaks and repairing the HVAC components affected by the impact.

PROJECT PHASES

THE ROAD BACK.

01 / ARRIVAL

Non-Runner Recovery

The G80 arrived unable to drive under its own power, setting up the first challenge: safely recovering the car and beginning the diagnosis.

02 / REPAIR

Cooling & Lighting

Headlight replacement and cooling-system work move the project toward a complete front end and a car that can be confidently tested.

03 / SYSTEMS

HVAC & Final Checks

Impact-related HVAC damage, leak verification and remaining system checks are part of wrapping up the mechanical recovery.
