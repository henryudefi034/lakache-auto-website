CURRENT PROJECT / GENESIS G80

ARRIVED DEAD.
NOW IT RUNS.

The Genesis arrived as a non-runner after collision damage. The first job was figuring out why. Once diagnosed and fixed, it came back to life — and that was only the beginning of the rebuild.

MODEL2017 Genesis G80
ARRIVALNon-Runner
CURRENT STATUSRunning
PROJECTCollision Recovery
01 / THE ARRIVAL

IT WOULDN'T
RUN.

The G80 arrived unable to run under its own power. Before worrying about making the car look complete, the first challenge was understanding why the car would not run and determining what the collision had left behind.

That diagnosis became the first win of the project. The cause was identified, repaired and the Genesis was brought back to life. From that point forward, the project changed from recovering a non-runner to rebuilding a running collision-damaged car.

02 / THE TURNING POINT

BACK TO
LIFE.

Getting the engine running changed the direction of the entire build. It confirmed that the Genesis had a foundation worth continuing with and allowed attention to move toward the remaining collision-related repairs.

Running did not mean finished. The front of the car still needed work, and the cooling, lighting and HVAC systems all had to be addressed before the G80 could be considered properly recovered.

03 / THE REBUILD

WHAT COMES NEXT.

With the non-running problem solved, the build now moves through the systems affected by the collision one stage at a time.

01 / DIAGNOSIS

Non-Runner Solved

Figure out why the G80 arrived as a non-runner, correct the problem and get the car running again.

02 / LIGHTING

Headlight Installation

Install and test the replacement headlight as the damaged front end continues coming back together.

03 / COOLING

Vacuum & Refill

Vacuum-fill the cooling system with fresh coolant, verify the system and check carefully for leaks.

04 / HVAC

Impact Damage

Replace the HVAC component damaged in the collision and continue working through the remaining systems.

04 / CURRENT STATUS

THE BUILD
CONTINUES.

The biggest early question has already been answered: the Genesis runs. Now the focus is on completing the supporting systems and working through the collision damage correctly rather than rushing toward a finished appearance.

This page will continue to evolve as each stage is completed, documenting the G80 from its non-running arrival through the final recovery.
