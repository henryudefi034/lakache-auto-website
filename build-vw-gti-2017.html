WHERE IT

Before the newer projects, the white Mk7 GTI was the car at the beginning of the Lakache Auto story — a fully restored 2017 GTI Autobahn and the foundation for what the garage became.

01 / THE BEGINNING

ONE CAR.
A BIGGER IDEA.

The white Mk7 GTI became more than a finished project. It was the early proof of the idea behind Lakache Auto: take on the work, learn through the process and document the story around the car.

That approach eventually expanded into more rebuilds, DIY repairs and long-term ownership stories. The projects changed, but the idea that started here stayed the same.

02 / THE CONNECTION

MK7 TO
MK8.

Years later, the blue 2024 GTI Autobahn brought the story back to the same nameplate in a newer generation. That made the finished Mk8 more meaningful than a standalone rebuild.

The two cars became bookends in the Lakache Auto story: the white Mk7 where the journey started and the blue Mk8 showing how far the builds had progressed.

03 / THE LEGACY

THE FIRST
CHAPTER.

Not every important project has to be the newest, most expensive or most complicated. The Mk7 matters because it represents the beginning — the car connected to the earliest chapter of Lakache Auto.

As the garage grows, this GTI remains part of the identity behind the channel and the website: cars, builds and the life created around them.
