Lakache Auto is about the cars we build, the cars we own, and everything we learn along the way. From bringing damaged cars back to life to documenting long-term ownership, the goal is simple — share the real experience behind the cars.

01 / THE BEGINNING

BUILT FROM A
PASSION FOR CARS.

What began with DIY projects and ownership experiences grew into something bigger — salvage rebuilds, repairs, diagnostics, long-term reviews and the stories behind each car.

WHERE THE STORY BEGAN

Lakache Auto started with a simple idea: document the experience of working on cars and share the process honestly.

There are no perfect builds here. Problems happen. Diagnoses can be wrong. Projects take unexpected turns. That's part of what makes the journey worth documenting.

The cars may change and the projects may get bigger, but the approach stays the same: do the work, understand the problem, learn from the experience and share the story.
