Lakache Auto documents what happens when a passion for cars turns into real projects — buying them, rebuilding them, maintaining them, living with them and sharing what the process actually teaches.

01 / THE BEGINNING

BUILT FROM A
PASSION FOR CARS.

What began with DIY work and ownership experiences grew into salvage rebuilds, diagnostics, repairs and long-term stories — all centered on the real experience behind the machine.

THE CAR THAT STARTED THE STORY

The white 2017 Golf GTI Autobahn represents the beginning of the Lakache Auto story — before the newer projects, bigger repairs and growing garage.

The idea was simple: work on the cars, understand the problems and document the experience honestly. Not every repair goes perfectly. Diagnoses change. Projects take unexpected turns.

That reality is part of the story. The cars have changed, but the approach has not: do the work, learn from it and share what actually happened.
