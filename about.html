01 / WHERE IT STARTED

ONE GTI.
A BIGGER IDEA.

Before the growing project garage, documented rebuilds and enthusiast inventory, there was a simple idea: work on the cars, understand the problems and share what actually happened.

THE CAR THAT STARTED THE STORY
2017 VOLKSWAGEN GTI AUTOBAHN

The beginning of Lakache Auto.

The white Mk7 GTI represents the beginning of the Lakache Auto story—before the newer projects, bigger repairs and expanding garage.

The approach was simple: do the work, learn from it and document the experience honestly. Diagnoses can change. Repairs can fight back. Projects can take unexpected turns.

That reality became part of the identity. The cars have changed, but the idea has not.

04 / THE PHILOSOPHY

CARS
BUILD LIFE.

Cars can create much more than transportation. They create projects, challenges, memories, conversations, skills and communities.

Lakache Auto is about that larger experience—the work in the garage, the lessons from ownership, the people you meet and the life that happens around the cars.

The goal is not perfection. It is to keep learning, keep building and keep telling the story.
