Privacy Policy

How Lakache Auto handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

When you submit a vehicle or business inquiry, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, location, destination country, buyer type, vehicle interest, shipping arrangements, and message. We use this information to respond to your request and assess whether a proposed transaction may be appropriate.

Inquiry form provider

Our website uses Formspree to process certain inquiry forms and deliver messages to our business email. Information entered into those forms is transmitted to Formspree for processing under its own policies. Do not include payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive documents in the initial inquiry.

How information is used

We use inquiry details to communicate about vehicles, documentation, eligibility, logistics questions and related business requests. We do not represent that submitting a form creates a purchase agreement.

Website hosting and third-party services

The website may rely on third-party hosting, linked video platforms and independent vehicle-history services. Those services may process technical data or apply their own privacy policies when you use them. External links are not controlled by Lakache Auto.

Sharing and retention

We may share relevant inquiry details with service providers needed to process communications or as legally required. We do not publish inquiry details on vehicle pages. Inquiry messages may be retained for business correspondence and recordkeeping; a fixed retention period has not yet been established.

Your questions and requests

For questions about your inquiry information, requests for correction, or deletion requests where applicable, email info@lakacheautos.com. We will review requests subject to applicable recordkeeping and legal obligations.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised as website features and business practices change. Last updated October 17, 2026.
