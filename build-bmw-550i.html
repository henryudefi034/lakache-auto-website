COMPLETED BUILD / 2016 BMW 550i M SPORT

A SECOND LIFE
FOR THE V8.

Side-swipe damage, a destroyed headlight and a surprisingly affordable set of matching body panels turned this 445-horsepower BMW into a hands-on Lakache Auto rebuild.

MODEL2016 BMW 550i
ENGINE4.4L Twin-Turbo V8
POWER445 HP
STATUSCompleted Build

THE BUILD
STORY.

The 550i came with heavy side-swipe damage, including damaged doors, fender and headlight. Rather than treating the car as a collection of expensive problems, the rebuild started with a simple strategy: find the right matching parts and save where it made sense.

Matching doors and a fender were sourced through Marketplace for $600. The soft-close mechanisms and wiring were transferred, the replacement panels were fitted and aligned, and the damaged LED headlight became its own repair project instead of simply being replaced at full used-part cost.

PROJECT HIGHLIGHTS

THREE PARTS.
ONE CAR.

A rebuild centered on smart parts sourcing, hands-on repair and bringing a powerful German sedan back without turning every damaged component into a replacement bill.

01 / BODY

Marketplace Panels

Matching front and rear doors plus a fender were sourced for $600, followed by mechanism transfers, wiring work and panel alignment.

02 / HEADLIGHT

Repair, Don't Replace

The damaged LED headlight was opened and repaired with a replacement lens rather than accepting the cost of another complete assembly.

03 / FINISHED CAR

Back to the Road

The completed 550i returns as a 445-horsepower twin-turbo V8 sedan — and the final review asks whether the rebuild was worth taking on.
