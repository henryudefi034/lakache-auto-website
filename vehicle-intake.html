WHAT MAKES A STRONG SUBMISSION

THE FACTS
FIRST.

Accurate vehicle information

Year, exact model and trim, current mileage, location and VIN when available.

Honest history

Tell us about title status, accidents, previous repairs and anything a buyer or mechanic should know.

Current mechanical condition

Running status, warning lights, leaks, needed service and non-working equipment are useful—not reasons to hide the car.

A realistic price expectation

Knowing what you hope to receive helps determine quickly whether continuing the conversation makes sense.

Good documentation

Service history, repair records, receipts and clear photos can become important during the next stage of review.
